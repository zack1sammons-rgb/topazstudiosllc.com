1. Who we are

Topaz Studios LLC (“Topaz Studios”, “we”, “us”) is a Utah limited liability company at 882 W 700 N, Provo, UT 84601, United States. We write, film and edit short-form video for brands and apps, and we run this website.

The BoomKit creator app is run by BoomKit LLC, a separate company, under its own privacy policy and terms.

2. This website

  • topazstudiosllc.com uses no cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, third-party scripts or third-party fonts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • When you email us, we receive your address and your message and use them to reply. Our domain’s email forwarding runs through Namecheap and arrives in a Google Gmail inbox.

3. Video clients

  • For a video project we keep the contact details, briefs, scripts, footage, invoices and messages the project needs.
  • We use them to make and deliver the work, to bill for it and to keep business and tax records.
  • We share project files only with the people working on that project, such as an editor or an actor, and with the services that store and send them (for example Google Drive and Gmail).

4. How long we keep it

  • Emails and project files: for as long as we work together, then as long as business and tax records require.
  • Server logs: GitHub keeps and overwrites them on its own schedule.

5. Your choices and rights

Ask us for a copy of your information, a correction or deletion by emailing zack@topazstudiosllc.com. We aim to answer within 30 days. Residents of California and some other states have specific privacy rights; we honor these requests for everyone.

6. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new date.
