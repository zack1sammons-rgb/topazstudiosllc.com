Questions

What’s an ad disclosure?

It’s the label that tells viewers a post is paid. Every campaign post needs it: “#ad” or “Paid partnership with [brand]” in the caption, TikTok’s commercial content setting turned on, and Instagram’s Paid Partnership label where Instagram offers it. The app gives you a caption line to copy. A post without the label doesn’t count.

Do I need followers?

No. There’s no follower minimum, and a new account is fine. You post from a TikTok or Instagram account you own and register it in the app for each campaign. We never ask for your password.

When do I get paid?

Daily pay lands in your Boomkit wallet as soon as a post qualifies. View pay lands every Monday for the week before. You can cash out each payment 14 days after it lands; that window is how we keep fake views out of pay.

Is it guaranteed?

No. Spots on every campaign are limited, and joining the waitlist doesn’t guarantee you one. What you earn depends on posting and on counted views, and most videos never reach 100,000 views. We say the same in our Terms, under “Estimates and no guarantees”.

How often do I need to post?

Daily pay comes from your first qualifying post each day. If you go 5 days without a post, you come off the campaign. If none of your first 20 posts on a campaign reaches 10,000 counted views, we give the spot to someone else.

Does it cost anything?

No. You never pay to join or to stay on a campaign. We never ask for passwords or ID numbers on this form, and nobody from Boomkit will ask for your social media passwords.
